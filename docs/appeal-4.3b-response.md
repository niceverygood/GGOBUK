Dear App Review Team,

Thank you for the detailed feedback on our submission. We respectfully request reconsideration of the 4.3(b) determination and would like to provide additional context that we believe was not fully reflected in the initial review.

**1. Saju (사주명리학) is a distinct Korean cultural practice, not Western astrology**

Saju is a 1,500-year-old Korean metaphysical framework based on the precise lunar-solar calendar of birth (year/month/day/hour pillars, expressed as 8 Heavenly Stems and Earthly Branches). It is structurally and culturally distinct from horoscopes, zodiac signs, palm reading, or tarot — which appear to be the categories Guideline 4.3 primarily addresses.

There is no overlap in calculation method, interpretation framework, or cultural context. We would respectfully note that the dominant Saju apps on the Korean App Store (e.g., "사주아이", "포스텔러") serve a Korea-specific cultural need, comparable to how 一-柱-命-理 apps exist in the Japanese and Chinese markets without being grouped with Western astrology apps.

**2. 꼬북점 is a persistent companion experience, not a static fortune lookup**

The majority of apps in the category Apple references generate one-time text outputs from user input. 꼬북점 is fundamentally different in structure:

- **Original IP character (꼬북이)**: A turtle character with four distinct accessory-based personas (꼬북 / 도사 / 무당 / 보살), each with its own voice, tone, and conversational style. This is original character design, not stock zodiac graphics.
- **Persistent AI conversation**: Users maintain ongoing chat sessions with their chosen persona. The character remembers prior conversations and the user's life context, powered by Anthropic Claude.
- **Lifetime timeline (대운)**: Users see their entire life mapped across 10-year fate cycles — a feature absent in fortune-telling apps that focus on daily or yearly readings.
- **Relationship graph**: Users can register family members, friends, partners and analyze compatibility and dynamics across multiple people simultaneously — not a single-user reading.
- **Interactive tortoise shell UI**: The signature interface presents the eight Saju pillars on a tactile shell that the user explores by tapping each panel, rather than scrolling a static result page.

**3. Differentiation summary**

| Typical category app | 꼬북점 |
|---|---|
| One-time text output | Ongoing AI companion with memory |
| Generic zodiac/horoscope art | Original IP character with 4 personas |
| Single-user reading | Multi-person relationship analysis |
| Daily / yearly forecast | Lifetime 10-year cycle timeline |
| Tab-based menu UI | Interactive tortoise shell metaphor |

**4. Request**

We would be grateful if a reviewer could re-examine the app with the persona chat (Tab 2) and relationship graph (Tab 4) features in mind — these are the primary differentiators and may not have been fully exercised in the initial review. We are happy to provide a demo account or any additional materials that would assist the re-review.

We have invested significant effort to build a culturally specific, IP-driven experience rather than a generic fortune-telling utility, and we believe 꼬북점 offers a meaningfully new experience for Korean-speaking users.
